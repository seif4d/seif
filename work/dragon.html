From context
to a clearer product.

The challenge

Many learners collect lessons without crossing the gap into complete projects, review and contribution.

The direction

A venture concept exploring project-based learning, shared feedback and a visible path from first exercise to finished product.

One product decision

Organize learning around milestones, peer feedback and visible output so progress is demonstrated through work.
